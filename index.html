Security Center

Simple rules to stay protected from modern cyber scams.

🎣

Phishing Protection

Verify domains carefully. Never enter passwords, OTPs or card information through suspicious links.

💳

UPI Safety

You never need to enter your UPI PIN to receive money. Verify every payment request before approving it.

💼

Fake Job Scams

Genuine employers generally don't demand money for registration, training or guaranteed job offers.

🤖

AI Scam Safety

Verify urgent voice/video requests independently. AI-generated voices and videos can impersonate trusted people.
